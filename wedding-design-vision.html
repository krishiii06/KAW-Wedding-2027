Hi! I'm planning my own wedding and working directly with decor vendors in Hyderabad. There are seven events between December 2026 and February 2027.

I already have the vision, references and rough sketches for each event. I'm looking for a designer to stitch them into one cohesive look per event, scale each element to the real venue using dimensions I provide, and bring their creative eye. That includes telling me when an idea won't work in the space or could look better.

What I need at the end is a clear view of every element that a decor vendor can quote and build from as is. The vendors handle materials, quantities, fabrication and costing themselves. The look throughout is minimalist, elegant and ethereal, rooted in Telugu tradition.
